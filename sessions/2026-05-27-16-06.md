## Context Update
The startup is pivoting from competing in the saturated English-language desktop voice-to-text market toward a blue ocean strategy targeting underserved non-English markets (Polish, Ukrainian, Russian) with mobile-first use cases. Rather than competing with well-funded players like Whisperflow ($80M raised, $700M valuation), they're seeking a niche where existing solutions don't focus. The core product direction is shifting from developer tools (coding assistants) to everyday mobile communication scenarios: smart keyboards that not only transcribe but AI-enhance spoken input, voice-to-text for messengers, and potentially translation features. The team is prioritizing speed to market with a cloud-based MVP over privacy-focused on-device processing, targeting non-technical users who will pay for convenience.
